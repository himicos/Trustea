How it works
Three steps.
Runs for decades.

Trustea replaces paper filings and lawyer phone calls with verifiable on-chain logic that executes automatically — with a human in the loop.

01
Write rules in plain English
No legal jargon. The AI translates compound conditions — age milestones, enrollment, conviction status — into enforceable on-chain logic.
02
AI trustee monitors 24/7
Model-agnostic agent checks conditions continuously and proposes distributions. Grantor keeps a configurable veto window on every action.
03
Executes on-chain, permanently
Every decision is Seal-encrypted and stored on Walrus via MemWal. Private, verifiable, reconstructable 50 years from now.

Features
Everything a traditional trust does —
and what it can't.
AI Rule Translator
12 real-world patterns: age milestones, HEMS, incentive matching, bankruptcy protection, generation-skipping. Works with Claude, GPT, or local LLMs.
Human Override
Grantor or trust protector can veto any AI-proposed action within a configurable window (default 48h). You stay in control — always.
Seal Encryption
Trust documents and AI decisions identity-encrypted via Seal. Only authorized parties can decrypt — not even Trustea.
Soulbound NFTs
Non-transferable beneficiary NFTs encode allocation rights and access rules directly into the wallet. No counterparty risk.
Real-World Asset Tokens
Represent real estate, securities, vehicles as soulbound tokens with on-chain valuation tracking. The full estate — on-chain.
Permanent Memory
Every agent decision stored as Seal-encrypted memories on Walrus via MemWal. Semantic recall across sessions — context that builds over decades.
