> Here's the problem with hiring most lawyers. First of all, you have no clue how much you're gonna spend. You know the price per hour, you don't know the final cost until it's too late. Throughout the case you're left in the dark, constantly chasing your lawyer for an update. And let's be honest — your lawyer doesn't actually have any skin in the game. They make money regardless of the outcomes of your case.
>

>

>

>

They can now offer their clients fixed pricing, predictable quality, and real-time case visibility.

> It sounds simple. But in law, it's revolutionary.

## What Makes It Work

1. **Starts with YOU, not with THEM.** "Here's the problem with hiring lawyers" — the viewer is the subject, not the company.
2. **Personal story early.** "$3,000/hr — he said watch me." Real, specific, funny. Breaks the corporate pitch tone.
3. **Both sides of the pain.** Client suffers AND lawyer suffers. The system is broken for everyone. Nobody is the villain.
4. **One devastating stat.** "80% can't afford a lawyer." One number. Not three. Not a chart. One gut punch.
5. **Analogy to solved problem.** Taxis → Uber. Instant understanding. "Oh, they're doing THAT but for law."
6. **"Technology alone won't fix it."** This is the smartest line. Separates them from every "we built an app" pitch. They're building a MODEL, not a tool.
7. **Solution in one sentence.** "Unified brand + back-office + tech = fixed pricing, quality, visibility." Anyone can understand this.
8. **Tagline is earned.** "Sounds simple. In law, it's revolutionary." Works because they spent 80 seconds proving why it's hard.
