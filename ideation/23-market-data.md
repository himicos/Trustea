## Beneficiary Pain Points (documented)

1. **Slow/withheld distributions** — typical trust wind-down: 12–18 months
2. **Lack of transparency** — trustees withholding financial statements
3. **Excessive/opaque fees** — beneficiaries can challenge but rarely do (cost of litigation)
4. **Conflicts of interest** — bank trustees placing proprietary funds
5. **Unresponsive trustees** — chronic secrecy, poor communication

Sources: Keystone Law, PCS Law WNYC, Pendleton Square Trust
