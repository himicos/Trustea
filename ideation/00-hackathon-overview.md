## Judging Criteria (Core Tracks)
| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| **Real-World Application** | **50%** — meaningful problem-solving, market relevance, long-term value |
| **Product & UX** | **20%** — quality, usability, polish |
| **Technical Implementation** | **20%** — technical quality, reliability, meaningful Sui integration |
| **Presentation & Vision** | **10%** — storytelling, clarity, long-term vision |

## What Makes a Strong Project
- Solves meaningful problems
- Polished UX
- Leverages Sui meaningfully (not just "deployed on Sui")
- Strong product thinking
- Long-term potential beyond hackathon
- **NOT just technical demos** — real products matter

- Must be built during May 7 – June 21, 2026
