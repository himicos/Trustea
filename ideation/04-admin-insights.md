# Admin Insights (from Telegram)

## Judging Criteria — SAME for ALL tracks
The 20/50/20/10 weighting applies universally:
- Product & UX: 20%
- Real-World Application: 50%
- Technical Implementation: 20%
- Presentation & Vision: 10%

**Walrus and DeepBook sponsor tracks use the SAME criteria as core tracks.**

## Testnet Features
- **Encouraged** to integrate newer testnet-only features
- BUT: 50% of prize requires mainnet deployment
- If project depends on testnet-only features → must wait for mainnet launch to unlock remaining 50%
- **Implication**: Stick to mainnet-available tech if possible, or be prepared to wait

## Presentation & Vision (10%) — Clarified
- Focus: **how clearly you communicate your project**
  - Quality of demo/presentation
  - Storytelling
  - Explaining the problem and solution well
  - Long-term vision/potential
- Social traction can provide context around adoption but **NOT the primary focus**
- This means: great demo video + compelling narrative > Twitter followers

## Fresh Teams Welcome (Admin Quote)
> "We totally understand the concern, but don't be discouraged from participating. This year's judging criteria is also different from previous years, with a much heavier focus on real-world application, meaningful problem-solving, and long-term value. A big part of the evaluation is still focused on the actual progress and implementation completed during the hackathon period itself. We've also seen completely fresh teams and first-time builders do extremely well in past hackathons!"

**Key signal**: Fresh teams CAN win. Judging shifted TOWARD real-world application this year (50% weight). Implementation during hackathon period matters most. This favors Trustea — real problem, real market, built from scratch during the period.

## Strategic Takeaways
1. **Build on mainnet-ready tech** → deploy before winners announced → get 100% upfront
2. **Judging is product-heavy across ALL tracks** → even Walrus track rewards real-world application at 50%
3. **Demo video is critical** — it's the main vehicle for the 10% Presentation score
4. **No special sauce for sponsor tracks** — same criteria, so pick track based on fit, not judging differences
5. **Fresh teams welcome** — admin explicitly encourages first-time builders, judging favors real-world value over track record
