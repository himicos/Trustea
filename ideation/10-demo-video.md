## What We Do NOT Say

These claims are tempting but wrong or misleading:

- "No lawyers needed" — wrong. The legal trust document still requires an attorney.
- "Replaces trustees" — misleading. We replace the administration function, not the legal role. A licensed admin trustee may still be required.
- "$2 to create a trust" — misleading in isolation. $2 is the on-chain cost. The legal setup is still $1k-$10k.
- "35 trillion dollar market" — overestimate. $13T in private trusts, $15.5B admin market. Use correct numbers.
- "Universities already issue diplomas as NFTs" — premature. Some pilots exist but it's not standard. Say "as soulbound credentials become standard" (future tense).
- "Zero third parties see your data" — nuanced. The LLM inference sees prompts transiently. What's stored (MemWal, Walrus) is encrypted. Say "the permanent record is encrypted and private."
- "Fully automated trust fund" — the 48-hour override means a human is always in the loop. Say "AI-assisted" not "fully automated."

## What We DO Say

These are accurate and powerful:

- "The legal structure stays. The expensive administration layer doesn't."
- "$15,000 to $30,000 a year on a million-dollar trust — for rule-based work."
- "Every agent decision is encrypted and stored permanently on Walrus."
- "The human can override any AI action within a configurable window."
- "The same directed trust framework used by trust companies in 17 US states."
- "Beneficiaries can request distributions. Grantors can approve or deny."
- "The trust knows what it owns, what it's worth, and who it belongs to — on-chain."
- "65 integration tests passing against live Sui testnet right now."

## Production Notes

### Shooting
- Location: any financial district / modern city area with glass buildings
- Camera: iPhone Pro stabilized (gimbal or tripod)
- Both presenters in smart casual (not suits, not hoodies — founders look)
- Golden hour for closing vision segment

### Screen Recording
- Clean desktop, no clutter, light mode (matches brand)
- Large cursor, deliberate clicks
- Pre-populate trust with data (3 beneficiaries, rules, documents, agent history)
- Browser zoom 125% for readability
- Record at 1080p minimum

### Editing
- Fast cuts in Act 1 (0.5–2s per shot)
- Steady for Act 3 demo (let people follow the UI)
- Cinematic for Act 4 (longer shots, breathe)
- Background music: corporate/tech, builds in Act 1, drops for demo, swells for close
- No stock footage watermarks — use Pexels, Unsplash video (free)

### Anna's Role
- Co-presenter with equal screen time
- She explains: UX, privacy, beneficiary experience, future vision
- University student perspective adds credibility for University Award
- Natural delivery — rehearsed but not scripted word-for-word

### Key Principles
1. **First 10 seconds = visual pattern interrupt** (no talking, just energy)
2. **First 30 seconds = "this is a real problem with real money"**
3. **The AI translation is the hero moment** — spend time here
4. **Never claim more than the code does** — judges will check
5. **Close with quiet confidence** — "Trust fund administration, on-chain." Not "please vote for us."
