# Demo Video — Trustea (≤5 min)

## Format
- **Short film + screen demo hybrid**
- Full body shots, faces, walking through financial district
- Fast dynamic speech, two presenters alternating (Nik + Anna)
- MacBook screen share for live demo section
- First 30 seconds = hook that sticks among 100+ projects

## Shot-by-Shot Breakdown

### ACT 1: THE WORLD (0:00–0:45)

**[0:00–0:10] HOOK — Financial District Energy**
- Drone/wide shot: financial district skyline (NYC/London/Singapore vibes)
- Quick cuts: busy people in suits, stock tickers, glass towers
- Time-lapse: day→night→day (passage of time)
- *No voiceover yet. Just music + ambient city sound. Building tension.*

**[0:10–0:25] TRUST FUNDS — The Invisible Machine**
- Voiceover (Nik, walking through city, full body, fast pace):
  > "Every year, $35 trillion sits in trust funds. Wealth passed from generation to generation. Rules. Conditions. Gatekeepers."
- Quick cuts: paperwork stacks, lawyer offices, signatures, family photos aging
- Visual: time flowing — documents yellowing, kids growing up
  > "Get the money when you turn 25. Lose it if you drop out. Release it when she graduates."

**[0:25–0:35] THE PROBLEM — Pattern Break #1**
- Camera stops. Nik faces camera directly. Slower delivery for contrast:
  > "And the people enforcing these rules? They charge 1 to 2 percent of everything. Every year. For decades. For doing what a program could do in milliseconds."
- Beat. Let it land.
  > "The trust industry hasn't changed in 100 years."

**[0:35–0:45] THE SHIFT — Tech Is Here**
- Energy picks back up. Walking again, faster cuts:
  > (Anna, walking from opposite direction, meets Nik):
  > "Smart contracts. AI agents. Verifiable storage. The technology to automate trust administration isn't coming — it's here. It's just that nobody built it. Until now."

### ACT 2: TRUSTEA (0:45–1:15)

  > "You write rules in plain English."

**[1:15–1:20] TRANSITION**

## Production Notes

### Shooting
- Location: any financial district / modern city area with glass buildings
- Camera: iPhone Pro is fine if stabilized (gimbal)
- Both presenters in smart casual (not suits, not hoodies — founders look)
- Golden hour for the closing vision segment

### Screen Recording
- Clean desktop, no clutter, dark mode or light mode (match brand)
- Large cursor, visible clicks
- Pre-populate some data so the demo flows fast
- Record at 1080p minimum

### Editing
- Fast cuts in Act 1 (0.5–2s per shot)
- Steady for Act 3 demo (let people follow the UI)
- Cinematic for Act 4 (longer shots, breathe)
- Background music: corporate/tech, builds in Act 1, drops for demo, swells for close
- No stock footage watermarks — use free sources (Pexels, Unsplash video)

### Anna's Role in Video
- Co-presenter with equal screen time
- She explains UX, privacy, credentials, and the future vision
- Authentic university student perspective adds credibility for University Award
- Natural bilingual energy is fine if it comes through — shows real team

### Key Principles
1. **First 10 seconds = visual pattern interrupt** (no talking, just energy and scale)
2. **First 30 seconds = "holy shit this is a real problem"** moment
3. **"Why didn't anyone do this before?"** must be the subconscious reaction by 0:45
4. **Demo is clean and fast** — no fumbling, no "um", pre-rehearsed
5. **Close with confidence** — "Try it" not "please consider our project"
